# Third-party notices

CryptDrive itself is released under the [MIT License](LICENSE). Its builds
include third-party software under their own licenses, all compatible with
MIT for this use:

| Component | License |
|-----------|---------|
| [rclone](https://github.com/rclone/rclone) (the encryption and Google Drive engine) | MIT |
| [Go](https://go.dev/LICENSE) standard library and runtime | BSD-3-Clause |
| Go modules rclone depends on (about 220) | mostly MIT, Apache-2.0 and BSD; also ISC, MPL-2.0, UPL-1.0, CC0-1.0 and one LGPL-3.0 module |
| [Flutter](https://github.com/flutter/flutter/blob/master/LICENSE) and the Dart packages in [`app/pubspec.lock`](app/pubspec.lock) | mostly BSD-3-Clause and MIT |

The full license text of every Go module is in
[`app/assets/licenses/go.txt`](app/assets/licenses/go.txt), generated by
[`tools/generate-licenses.sh`](tools/generate-licenses.sh). The apps show
it, together with the licenses of Flutter and every Dart package, in
**Settings → Open-source licenses**.

The LGPL-3.0 module, [`github.com/cloudsoda/sddl`](https://github.com/cloudsoda/sddl),
comes with rclone's SMB backend in the desktop and mobile builds (not the
web build). Go links it statically: its source is available from its
repository, and this repository has everything needed to rebuild the app
against a modified version (`native/go.mod`, `native/build.sh`).
